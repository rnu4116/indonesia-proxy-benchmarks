## The Three Types You'll Hear About

Not all indonesia proxies are the same. Chosing the wrong type wastes money and wrecks your project before it starts.

**Datacenter proxies** come from server farms. They're fast, cheap, and stable. The downside: their IPranges are publicly known, so sites with serious anti-bot defenses (think TikTok or Shopee at peak hours) will sniff them out quickly.

**Residential proxies** route through real consumer ISPs in Indonesia. The IPs belong to actual home internet connections from providers like Telkomsel IndiHome, Biznet, First Media, MyRepublic. They look indistinguishable from regular Indonesian users. They're slower and pricier but pass even strict bot detection.

**ISP proxies** (sometimes called "static residential") are a hybrid. They're hosted in datacenters but registered under residential ISP ranges. You get datacenter sped with residential trust scores. They're a sweet spot for long-running sessions like ad verification or social media management.

Quick rule of thumb: scraping Tokopedia at scale? Residential. Verifying Indonesian Google rankings? Datacenter orISP. Running multiple e-commerce accounts? ISP.
